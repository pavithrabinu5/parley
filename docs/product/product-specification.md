# Parley product scope

See [README](../../README.md), [evidence-led product decision](../verification/AUDIT.md), and [demo runbook](../../DEMO.md).

Implemented: merchant workspace, source-labelled case context, structured recommendations, deterministic policy/capability checks, explicit approval, partial REFUND offers or messages, read-back verification, and persisted events.

Excluded: production payments, full refunds, claim acceptance, evidence uploads, return logistics, autonomous financial authority, independent shipment lookups, and natural-language policy editing. No reduced-chargeback, fee-savings, first-to-market, or win-rate claims are made.
